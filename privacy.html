Privacy Policy

TBML Fantasy Draft · Last updated September 14, 2026

TBML Fantasy Draft is a private companion app for one fantasy football league's live draft. It is not available to the general public and is not used to collect data for advertising or any purpose beyond running the league's draft.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and league role (owner or commissioner), used to log you in and identify your team.
  • Draft and roster data: the players you draft, your team's roster, your watchlist and notes, and your league's keeper/protect selections.
  • Basic device information needed to operate the app (e.g. push notification tokens, only if you enable draft-pick notifications).

We do not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or browsing history, and this app contains no advertising or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs.

How we use this information

Solely to run the league's draft: to show you your team and league's draft board, roster, watchlist, and standings, and (if enabled) to notify you when it's your turn to pick. Your email and name are visible to other members of your own league (this is a shared, private league tool), never to the public or to unrelated third parties.

Data storage

Data is stored on the league's own backend server and is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside running this league's draft.

Data retention & deletion

League and draft data is retained for the life of the league. To request deletion of your personal account data, contact jon.hokanson@outlook.com.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: jon.hokanson@outlook.com or visit our Support page.
