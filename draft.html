The TBML Draft

A 14-round offline snake draft, run live and tracked pick-by-pick — ten owners, one board, no waivers to bail anyone out afterward.

How draft day works

Every roster spot is filled through the draft itself — 14 picks per team, no separate bench-only rounds.

Rounds 1–12 — the live snake draft

Standard snake order, reversing each round. Ten picks per round, ordered by each team's draft position for the season.

Round 13 — Protect round

Each team names a protected pair before the draft. Whichever half of the pair gets drafted first — by anyone, including the owning team — stays exactly where it was picked. The other half is then guaranteed to that team at Round 13.

Round 14 — Keeper round

Each team's designated keeper is locked into their roster automatically, no pick required. A kept player can be kept for a maximum of two consecutive years, unless routed through Protects, which resets the clock.

🃏

10 teams

Head-to-head, standard (non-PPR) scoring. No dedicated WR slot — every wideout runs through a flex.

📜

14 roster spots

QB, RB, RB, W/T, W/T, W/R/T, K, DEF, and 6 bench spots — no IR slot. Full breakdown on the rules page.

📈

Instant grading

Once a team's draft is final, every pick is graded against ADP — starters vs. bench, steals vs. reaches, and a scarcity-weighted read on each roster's weakest position.

TBML Fantasy Draft, the app

The companion app for draft day — built specifically for this league.

Everything you need on draft day, from your phone

Follow the board live, search the available player pool, build and reorder your watchlist, check your roster against the league's starting lineup, and see your keeper & protect picks placed directly on the board — no more juggling a spreadsheet and a browser tab.

  • Live draft board — on the clock, pick history, and an alert when it's your turn
  • Full player pool with search, filters, and ADP
  • Personal watchlist, reorderable by priority
  • Roster tracker against your league's real starting lineup
  • League-wide view of every team's roster and draft position
  • Commissioner tools: manage the draft, override or undo picks
