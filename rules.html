Rules & Scoring

Ten teams, standard (non-PPR) scoring, and a keeper system built around two rounds at the end of every draft.

Format

10 teams · Head-to-head · Offline draft, tracked live

Scoring

Standard (non-PPR) · Fractional & negative points enabled

Playoffs

Top 6 teams · Weeks 15–17 · No divisions, no reseeding

Roster & starting lineup

Every team carries 14 roster spots — 8 starters and 6 bench, with no IR slot:

  • 1 QB
  • 2 RB
  • 2 W/T (WR/TE flex)
  • 1 W/R/T (WR/RB/TE flex — open to all three positions since a 2025 rule change)
  • 1 K
  • 1 DEF
  • 6 BN (bench, any position)

There's no dedicated WR-only slot — every receiver's production runs through one of the flex spots, which is a real constraint on draft strategy compared to leagues that guarantee 2–3 WR starting spots.

Scoring

Offense

  • Passing: 0.04 pt/yard (25 yds/pt), 4 pts/TD, −1 per interception thrown
  • Rushing & receiving: 0.1 pt/yard (10 yds/pt), 6 pts/TD
  • 2-point conversion: 2 pts · Fumble lost: −2 pts · Return/fumble-return TD: 6 pts

No PPR line item exists in league settings — TBML is standard scoring, not PPR or half-PPR.

Kicking

  • FG 0–39 yds: 3 pts · FG 40–49 yds: 4 pts · FG 50+ yds: 5 pts · PAT made: 1 pt

Defense / Special Teams

  • Sack: 1 · Interception: 2 · Fumble recovery: 2 · TD: 6 · Safety: 2 · Blocked kick: 2
  • Points allowed: 10 pts (0 allowed) sliding down to −4 pts (35+ allowed)

Season mechanics

  • No waivers — a continual rolling free-agent list, first come first served, no weekly limit.
  • Trades: no season maximum, commissioner-reviewed, 1-day reject window. Trading window closes in late November. Draft-pick trades are not allowed.
  • Tie-breakers: (1) fractional points, (2) total regular-season points, (3) head-to-head, (4) coin toss.
  • Rule changes require majority approval — 6 of the 10 owners.

Keeper & Protect rules

  • Keeper: one player per team, kept for free into Round 14. Maximum two consecutive years kept — unless the player is routed through Protects and not drafted, which resets the clock.
  • Protect: each team names a pair of players before the draft. Whichever half of the pair is drafted first — by any team — stays exactly where it was picked. The other half is then guaranteed to the protecting team at Round 13.

League history & special rules

Legacy Fund: a bonus payout, eligible only for a franchise that wins back-to-back titles. See the full championship record — no current franchise has ever actually repeated, so the fund has never been claimed.

The "Regan Rule" (introduced 2025): if the reigning champion doesn't get the trophy engraved with the winner's name by draft day, they swap their 1st-round pick with the previous season's last-place finisher.

Entry fee: $150/team (increased from the original $125). Payout structure was revised in 2025.
