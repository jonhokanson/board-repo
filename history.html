Championship History

Every champion and every "toilet" (last-place finisher) since the league's first season in 2008.

Nobody has ever repeated

Across all 18 completed seasons (2008–2025), no current franchise has won back-to-back titles — which means the league's Legacy Fund, payable only to a repeat champion, has never once been claimed.
