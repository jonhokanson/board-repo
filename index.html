Ted Brown Memorial League

Ten teams. One trophy.
Nineteen years of grudges.

TBML is a private, 10-team standard-scoring fantasy football league running continuously since 2008. This site covers the league's rules, its full championship history, the annual draft, and the TBML Fantasy Draft companion app.

What's in the league

Standard (non-PPR) scoring, no waivers, no dedicated WR slot — TBML runs a distinctive format that rewards deep rosters and sharp keeper decisions.

🏆

Real stakes, real history

Every champion and every last-place "toilet" finish since 2008 is on record — including a Legacy Fund bonus that has never once been claimed by a repeat champion.

📋

Keeper & Protect rules

Each team keeps one player into Round 14 for free and protects a pair of players guaranteed no later than Round 13 — a system that rewards long-term roster building.

📱

Draft day, on your phone

The TBML Fantasy Draft app keeps every owner on top of the board, the available player pool, and their watchlist — right from a phone on draft day.

Reigning champion: Old No 7

Old No 7 enters the next draft as the two-time defending... well, back-to-back-eligible champion — except no TBML franchise has ever actually repeated. The Legacy Fund is still waiting for its first winner. See the full championship record for every season since 2008.
