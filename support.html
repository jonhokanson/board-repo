Contact

For app support, account access, bug reports, or anything about the league itself, reach out directly:

Email hokanson.jon@comcast.net

We aim to respond within a few days. Please include your team name and a short description of the issue.

App account issues

Locked out, need your login reset, or your team isn't showing up correctly? Email the address above — the league commissioner manages all owner accounts directly.

Bugs & feedback

Found something broken in the app, or have an idea for a feature? We read every message and this app is actively maintained for the league.

League questions

Rules, scoring, keeper/protect questions, or draft-day logistics — see the Rules & Scoring page first, then reach out if anything's unclear.

Privacy & data

To request deletion of your personal account data, or with any privacy question, see our Privacy Policy or email us directly.
